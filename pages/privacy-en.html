PRIVACY POLICY

This page describes how personal information is handled on the official website of Kaito Kawasaki (kaitokawasaki.com, "this site").

This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand traffic. Google Analytics collects anonymous statistical data using cookies and similar technologies; it does not include information that identifies you personally. You can decline this data collection by disabling cookies in your browser settings.

For details, please see Google's Privacy Policy.

2. Contact

The contact links on this site simply open your own email application (mailto links). No information is submitted to or stored on this site's server through a form. Any message you send goes directly from your own email service to the listed contact address.

3. Sharing with Third Parties

Other than the statistical data collected by Google Analytics described above, this site does not share or provide any information to third parties.

4. Cookies

This site uses cookies for analytics purposes. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though doing so may affect some functionality of the site.

This policy may be revised from time to time without prior notice. The version published on this page at any given time is the one in effect.

For questions about this policy, please reach out via Contact.
