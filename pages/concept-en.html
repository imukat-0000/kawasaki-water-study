Kogei Realism工芸リアリズム

  • I

    Kogei (craft) is the practice of expressing an ecosystem that includes the human being.

  • II

    Within the relationship of material, environment, body, and perception, something actually rises into being. What is made is that emergence.

  • III

    This is not realism achieved through technical skill. It is an attitude that reconstructs the very conditions of existence itself.
