Artist

KAITO KAWASAKI
Metal-casting artistImono / ChuukinKOGEI REALISM

Kaito Kawasaki is not an artist who imitates life. He is an artist who, through kogei (craft), creates the place where life rises up.

Born in 2000 in Kure, Hiroshima Prefecture. Metal-casting artist (imono / chuukin). Completed the master's program in Metal Casting, Department of Crafts, Graduate School of Fine Arts, Tokyo University of the Arts, in 2026. Based in Tokyo.

A cast goldfish is placed inside a body of acrylic resin. The resin is neither an added material nor a protective shell — it functions as an environment: another form of water. Polishing, heat, oxygen, temperature. By precisely tuning these conditions, color is generated.

  • ExhibitionsSolo exhibition "Utsuroi, Tomeru" (Garari Showcase, Obuse, Nagano), plus international art fairs
