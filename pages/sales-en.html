About the Works

Every work is one of a kind. Goldfish of the same variety are cast from the same original mold, but differences in molding, casting temperature, and coloring give each piece its own individual character. The resin, too, takes on a different expression each time depending on the shape of the pour and the curing process, so no two works are identical.

Changes over time, such as shifts in the resin's tone, are considered part of the work — including the chance effects that occurred during its making — and as a rule no restoration is offered. If you have concerns, please feel free to reach out via Contact.
