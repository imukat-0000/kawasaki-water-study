Thread-Finned Deep-Sea Fish

イトヒキイワシ

Deep-sea fish (thread-finned)

In the deep sea, a condition where no light arrives, only the thread-like trailing fin carries information. Forging — hammering iron out into a thread — turns that fragility inside out, into the strength of metal.

Material
Iron
Technique
Forging (hammered metalwork), oil-quench blackening
