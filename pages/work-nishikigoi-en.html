2026 / Master's Thesis Work

Nishikigoi

錦鯉

Koi (carp)

Salon de Printemps Award

More than a hundred times the scale of the goldfish works, this is, for now, the largest piece. It was brought into being through constant negotiation with the physical limits of metal casting.

Material
Silver, copper, sahari, brass (aluminum-alloyed), shakudo, shibuichi
Technique
Vacuum-pressure casting, gravity casting, plaster mold, polishing, nikuiro oxidation, hido oxidation, sulfide patination, silver plating, resin encapsulation
Exhibited
74th Tokyo University of the Arts Graduation & Diploma Works Exhibition (2026)
Three-person exhibition ‘MATSURI INORU’, Spiral Garden, Shin-Marunouchi Building (Jun 8–Aug 17, 2026, extended)
