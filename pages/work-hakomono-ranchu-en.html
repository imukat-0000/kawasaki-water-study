Hakomono: Ranchu

箱物 蘭鋳

Goldfish (ranchu)

A goldfish taken out of water shifts into a different mode of existence, even while keeping the same form. What is lost is not its shape, but its condition.

Material
Silver, copper, brass (aluminum-alloyed)
Technique
Vacuum-pressure casting, plaster mold, polishing, silver plating, resin coating
Exhibited
17th Geidai Art Plaza Grand Prize Exhibition (2023) — Selected
