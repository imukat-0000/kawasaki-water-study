Gakkyo no Kiku (The Mad Painter’s Chrysanthemum)

画狂ノ菊

Chrysanthemum (based on Hokusai’s ‘Chrysanthemums’)

The petals curl back layer upon layer, recasting in metal the same spiral Hokusai gave his waves. The title continues the wordplay of ‘Gakkyo no Kaeru’, turning on Hokusai’s own artist’s name.
