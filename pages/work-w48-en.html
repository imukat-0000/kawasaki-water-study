Minogame (Moss-Shell Turtle)

蓑亀

Turtle

A minogame, wearing algae on its shell like a straw raincoat, is a form that grows precisely by not moving. The relief of the chased surface and the gradations of sulfide patination carve in the length of that stillness.

Material
Copper
Technique
Chasing (repoussé), polishing, sulfide patination, wax coating
